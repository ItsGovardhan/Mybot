Your privacy matters.

Here’s a straightforward guide to information the Bot may handle to provide giveaways and community stats.

01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of Giveaway+ (“we,” “us,” or “our”) may handle information when you use the Bot in Discord or visit this website. The Bot is an independent project and is not operated by Discord.

The Bot’s available features include giveaways and member message statistics. This policy describes information that may be needed to provide those features; it is not a guarantee about data practices that have not been verified in the deployed service.

02

Information the Bot may process

When the Bot runs in a Discord server, it may receive or store information needed to respond to commands and provide its features, including:

  • Discord server, channel, and member identifiers needed to associate activity with the correct community;
  • message-count totals or related activity counters used for member statistics and leaderboards;
  • giveaway configuration, such as a prize description, duration, message reference, entries, and winner information; and
  • command and interaction data made available to the Bot by Discord when someone uses a Bot feature.

The Bot’s message-stat feature is designed to count activity; the feature does not need to retain the text of ordinary messages to maintain those counters. Confirm this against the live code, logging, and hosting configuration before relying on that statement.

03

How information is used

Information may be used to operate and maintain the Bot; create and manage giveaway entries and draws; calculate, update, and display message statistics; respond to commands; troubleshoot errors; prevent abuse; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use message-count data to read or profile the contents of ordinary conversations. The Bot’s features should be used for their stated community purposes.

04

Storage and security

Information may be stored in the Bot operator’s configured database and on the infrastructure used to run the Bot. The actual storage provider, physical location, access controls, backups, and security measures depend on the deployment and must be confirmed by the operator.

No online service can promise perfect security. The operator should limit access to operational data, protect hosting credentials, and take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

05

When information may be shared

The Bot uses Discord to communicate with your server, so Discord processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The Bot operator may also rely on hosting or infrastructure providers to run the service, subject to the arrangements and safeguards actually configured by the operator.

Information may be disclosed if legally required, to protect the rights or safety of people or the service, or as part of a transfer of the service. This draft does not claim that any particular third-party provider is used beyond Discord and providers configured by the Bot operator.

06

How long information is kept

Information may be retained for as long as it is needed to provide Bot features, preserve active giveaway records, maintain community statistics, address operational issues, or meet legal obligations. The Bot’s database and backups determine how long records actually remain.

Leaving a server or removing the Bot may not automatically erase existing database backups or records. The operator should publish a clear deletion and retention process after verifying how the deployed service works.

07

Your choices and requests

You can stop interacting with the Bot and ask a server administrator to remove it from a server. Depending on the Bot’s deployed features, an administrator may be able to manage or reset message counters through staff commands.

For questions or requests about Bot-related information, contact the Bot operator using the official support channel. The operator may need enough details to locate a record and may need to verify a request before taking action. Applicable privacy rights vary by location.

08

About this website preview

This preview is a collection of static pages. It has no Discord login, forms, or website-to-bot connection. It does not intentionally save information submitted by visitors because there are no submission fields.

The stylesheet requests fonts from Google Fonts. When the website is served with internet access, the visitor’s browser may send ordinary connection information to Google to retrieve those font files. The hosting provider may also keep standard server logs depending on its configuration. Remove or self-host the external fonts and check hosting logs if you need to avoid those requests.

09

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the Bot’s features or data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Please review the policy from time to time.

If you have a privacy question or want to make a data request, contact the Bot operator through the official support channel. Add your real support or privacy contact before publishing this policy.
